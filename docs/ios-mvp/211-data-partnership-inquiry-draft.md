# Draft: 211 data partnership inquiry

Status: owner-ready draft only; **not sent**.

211 Canada publicly says 211 data can be supplied through APIs, map-ready files, or Excel after a request through its Data Sharing Portal, and that it has worked with government, academia, businesses and nonprofits. This establishes a route to ask, not a licence or entitlement to reuse the data.

The ordinary B.C. and Alberta website terms limit content use to personal, non-commercial purposes unless separately authorized and restrict automated copying or redistribution. Miller must not scrape or ingest provincial 211 listings without written permission. Saskatchewan scope, commercial use, attribution, caching, corrections and update terms all require confirmation.

## Inquiry draft

Subject: Western Canada service-data partnership inquiry for Miller Navigator

Hello,

I am developing Miller Navigator, an early-stage, privacy-minimizing workflow prototype for frontline social-service and healthcare workers in British Columbia, Alberta and Saskatchewan. It helps a worker interpret a multi-need request, distinguish services physically located in a community from regional services that serve it, and create a concise resource handoff pack. It is not a replacement for 211, case management, or clinical decision support.

I would like to understand whether a small commercial pilot could obtain authorized access to relevant 211 program data through an API or scheduled file feed. Could you advise on:

- the appropriate data-request or partnership process for B.C., Alberta and Saskatchewan;
- permitted commercial pilot and derived-workflow uses;
- available API/file formats and geographic/service-area fields;
- update cadence, record identifiers, correction flow and service levels;
- attribution, caching, retention and redistribution requirements;
- fees, minimum agreement terms and pilot constraints; and
- whether provider contact/access details may appear in a worker-created resource pack.

The prototype would retain 211 provenance, apply its own verification status, and would not store client identifiers or query transcripts. I would be happy to provide a short demo and data-flow diagram.

Thank you,

[Owner name]
