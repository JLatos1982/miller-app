# Five-user Miller Navigator pilot protocol

## Question

Can a frontline worker understand a synthetic practical situation, find useful verified options, review the pack, and initiate a handoff in under 60 seconds?

## Participants and session

Recruit approximately five people across addiction clinical work, social work, nursing, counselling, and outreach/community navigation. Participation is voluntary; use synthetic scenarios only and collect no client information.

Give each participant three scenarios: one familiar urban case, one multi-need transition, and one rural/regional pathway. Include both text and voice across the session. Start timing when Home appears and stop when the native Share Sheet or print controller opens with a pack the participant would hand over.

Suggested rural cases are Port Hardy withdrawal plus transport; Haida Gwaii addiction treatment; La Loche OAT with no local-clinic assumption; High Level withdrawal plus post-treatment housing; and Fort St. John post-treatment housing plus counselling.

## Measures

- completion time and tap count;
- completion or abandonment;
- useful first result and correct geography;
- whether local, serving, intake, virtual and province-wide roles were clear;
- resources removed, reordered or replaced before sharing;
- access facts participants had to verify elsewhere;
- confidence and usefulness on a short five-point response;
- preference and reason compared with the participant's usual 211, Google, bookmark or spreadsheet workflow.

Record no query transcript, name, client detail, PHI, or case history. Feedback must use bounded resource-correction categories.

## Honest acceptance gate

- at least 80% of representative tasks initiate a useful handoff within 60 seconds;
- zero private-data leakage and zero unsupported eligibility/availability claims;
- at least 90% correct local-versus-serves interpretation;
- every wrong or stale resource can be reported in seconds;
- participants can explain why Miller saved work—or say candidly that it did not.

For the same three scenarios, ask how the worker would ordinarily use 211, Google, or internal bookmarks/spreadsheets. Do not force identical interaction patterns. Miller's expected advantage is multi-need decomposition, source-backed sequencing, regional semantics and a ready-to-share pack. If it merely returns the same links, mark the scenario as not differentiated.
