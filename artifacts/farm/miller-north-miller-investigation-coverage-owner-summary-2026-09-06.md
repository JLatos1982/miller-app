# Miller North and Miller investigation / coverage summary

> Private owner summary. Publication-safe product changes were deployed separately; this file remains local-only.

## What changed

- Alberta's Indigenous Patient Safety Investigator and Advocate dossier now includes the first located role-specific aggregate activity measure: 321 correspondence items in 2025.
- The measure is explicitly bounded: it does not show unique complainants, accepted matters, completed reviews, recommendations, referrals, resolutions or outcomes.
- A source-backed comparison now describes selected complaint and accountability mechanisms in British Columbia, Alberta and Saskatchewan across 12 common fields without ranking provinces.
- Miller Practical Supports increased from 31 to 43 publication-safe records through 12 new records and three non-destructive enrichments.
- No Watching Now record was due on September 6, 2026. A targeted Patient Care Quality Review check found no material change, so no public freshness state was altered.

## Alberta outcome transparency

- **Publicly knowable:** mandate, access route, published limits, institutional placement, appointment/operation, learning-session evidence and 321 correspondence items in 2025.
- **Partially knowable:** intake activity, recommendation/referral functions, systemic-information function, annual reporting and operational follow-up.
- **Opaque in role-specific public reporting:** accepted cases, completed reviews and remedies/resolutions.
- **Parent-office boundary:** 2024–25 office-level process improvements cannot be attributed to the Indigenous Patient Safety Investigator and Advocate.
- **Indigenous-led evidence:** First Nations-serving announcement and learning-session sources were located, but no independent Indigenous-led outcome evaluation was found.

Primary next action: if the owner wants more than public web evidence can provide, review the prepared narrow information-request concept for aggregate, privacy-protecting role activity and recommendation follow-up records. Do not request complaint narratives or patient information.

## Farm learning

The new `Accountability Office Outcome Transparency Review` primitive is reusable for advocates, ombudspersons, complaint investigators, regulators and review bodies. It requires all ten expected reporting fields, preserves parent-office attribution boundaries, rejects private complaint fields and overclaiming, and records a stopping reason plus one bounded next action.

No new relationship type was needed. Existing evidence relationships were sufficient when paired with an explicit relationship-strength classification.
