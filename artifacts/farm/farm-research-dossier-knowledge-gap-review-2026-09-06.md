# Private Research Dossier Knowledge-Gap Review

These are bounded research questions, not adverse findings. No information requests were sent.

## New Roads Therapeutic Recovery Community

- **review_first / owner_review:** Should the men’s and women’s New Roads programs be represented as one Miller resource, two resources or a parent with two programs? The choice affects user-facing service identity and deduplication, not the underlying evidence.
- **review_next / official_source_follow_up:** Is there an official report comparing actual participation with the announced estimate of 330 people over three years? Operation is established, but current assembled sources do not measure performance against the announced participation estimate.

## Creekside Withdrawal Management Centre — Road to Recovery expansion

- **review_next / FOI_candidate:** What share, if any, of the nearly $154-million Road to Recovery envelope was allocated to the Creekside expansion? The public chain establishes program funding and a named service result but not resource-level attribution.
- **review_next / official_source_follow_up:** Are Creekside-specific post-expansion utilization or capacity measures publicly reported? Wider-program utilization should not be presented as Creekside-specific performance.

## Saskatchewan First Nations Health Ombudsperson Office — governance and funding

- **review_first / official_source_follow_up:** What complaint, resolution, systemic-recommendation and outcome measures are reported in the latest FNHO annual report? Operation is established, but activity-level evidence would materially improve implementation and outcome understanding.
- **review_next / FOI_candidate:** How was the $775,000 contribution agreement allocated among office activities? The proactive-disclosure record stops at recipient level.

## In Plain Sight — implementation and public-reporting accountability

- **review_first / FOI_candidate:** Is there a current comprehensive official status ledger covering all 24 recommendations? The current official page itself identifies a limitation in comprehensive recommendation-level reporting.
- **review_first / official_source_follow_up:** What is the current status and evidence date for each of the 18 recommendations not disaggregated in the assembled current sources? Silence cannot be converted into non-implementation, completion or failure.
- **review_next / web_research:** Which implementation claims have independent operational or outcome evaluation? Government activity reporting and outcome evidence are different evidence classes.
