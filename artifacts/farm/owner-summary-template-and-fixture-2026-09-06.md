## Template

Every substantial research run should generate:

1. **What changed** — the smallest factual delta from the prior known state.
2. **Why it matters** — practical decision relevance, without advocacy or causal inference.
3. **Strongest evidence** — the best primary/independent support.
4. **Uncertainty / caution** — conflicts, missing evidence, scope limits, and owner gates.
5. **Recommended next move** — one primary bounded action.
6. **Key metrics** — stable counts with explicit denominators.

The generator accepts validated structured data and produces Markdown. It does not research, score evidence, publish, or resolve review items.
