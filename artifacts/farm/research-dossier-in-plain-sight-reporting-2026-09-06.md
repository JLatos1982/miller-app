# Private Research Dossier — In Plain Sight — implementation and public-reporting accountability

> Private owner-review artifact. Not a publication decision.

## Owner synthesis

### What this is

In Plain Sight documented Indigenous-specific racism in British Columbia health care and issued 24 recommendations. This dossier assembles the formal response, recommendation-level implementation examples and the Province’s current limitation in reporting comprehensively across all 24 recommendations.

### What we learned

- Declaration Act Action 3.07 is the Province’s current formal implementation vehicle for the recommendations.
- Official sources document specific activity connected to recommendations 1, 2, 17, 22, 23 and 24, including governance, a complaint process, a standard, legislative reform and education tools.
- The current Action 3.07 page says the reporting framework does not yet capture comprehensive progress across all 24 recommendations.
- Later federal reporting identifies a $171.8-million Indigenous Health and Cultural Safety Fund connected to recommendation 17, but the amount is program-level and not attributable to the full recommendation set.
- The reporting limitation is a source-backed accountability finding; it is not evidence that all unreported recommendations were unimplemented.

### Why it matters

The case tests whether Farm can represent documented progress and a material reporting gap at the same time without converting incomplete public reporting into a claim of institutional failure.

### What remains unclear

- Is there a current comprehensive official status report covering all 24 recommendations?
- What is the latest recommendation-level status for the 18 recommendations not disaggregated in the assembled current reporting?
- Which reported activities have independent operational or outcome evidence beyond government progress descriptions?

### Recommended next move

Seek or request a current 24-recommendation status ledger with status date, responsible organization and evidence source for each recommendation.
