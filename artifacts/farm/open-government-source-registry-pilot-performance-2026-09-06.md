# Open-Government Source Registry Pilot Performance

## Result

The private registry expanded from 18 to 27 source families. Fourteen families were exercised; 13 produced meaningful evidence. The additions were B.C. Declaration Act reporting, B.C. Open Information, Alberta King's Printer, Alberta health advocates, CPSA, Saskatchewan Publications, Saskatchewan Human Rights Commission, Parliament of Canada and FNHO.

| Family group | Access | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| B.C. health authorities | reliable | Named service, operator, capacity and current operation |
| B.C. news archive | reliable | Dated commitments, funding and launches |
| B.C. annual reports/estimates | reliable PDF | Program funding and utilization |
| B.C. laws/health/Declaration reporting | reliable | Statute, reform process, progress and reporting limit |
| Alberta health advocates | reliable | Operating mandate, legal lens and limits |
| CPSA | reliable PDF | Regulator action and binding-status distinction |
| Saskatchewan Human Rights Commission | reliable | Anonymized legal process and resolution terms |
| Saskatchewan Publications | working_with_limits | Authoritative record found; text extraction unsuccessful |
| Parliament of Canada | reliable | Royal assent, enacted status and committee context |
| Indigenous Services Canada | reliable | Launch funding and intergovernmental relationship |
| Open Government Canada | working_with_limits | Grant/review discovery; direct grant access less stable |
| FNHO | reliable | First Nations governance, current operation and reporting |

## Registry gaps

- A stable Saskatchewan statute-text endpoint or documented retrieval fallback.
- Provincial/federal court judgment families with case-citation normalization.
- A legislative history adapter that distinguishes current consolidated text from bill-stage documents.

No low-authority family was added merely to increase coverage.
