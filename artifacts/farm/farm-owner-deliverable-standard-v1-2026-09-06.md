# Farm Owner Deliverable Standard v1

Every substantial Farm research run should produce three synchronized private outputs.

## 1. Owner Summary

- **What changed:** the newly assembled or verified facts.
- **Why it matters:** the decision relevance in plain language.
- **Strongest evidence:** the best three to five source-backed items.
- **Uncertainty / caution:** material boundaries, conflicts and attribution limits.
- **Recommended next move:** one primary action.
- **Key metrics:** a compact count of records, sources, edges, gaps and review items.

The owner summary should be readable without the technical artifacts.

## 2. Research Brief

- Executive summary
- Scope
- Timeline
- Findings
- Evidence
- Limitations
- Unresolved questions
- Sources

The brief must be neutral, shareable-quality and source-grounded, but remains private until an explicit publication decision.

## 3. Structured Dossier JSON

The machine-readable equivalent must include identity, source set, timeline, organizations, policy/law/governance, accountability, public money, real-world implementation, implementation evidence, follow-up, evidence graph, gaps, draft information-request concepts, owner assessment and research-completeness assessment.

## Required safeguards

- private owner-review scope;
- production and publication mutation counts fixed at zero for read-only runs;
- one or more source references for every material edge and timeline event;
- explicit policy-context, funding-attribution and implementation scopes;
- no private patient information or attempts to identify anonymous people;
- no legal advice, causal conclusion or financial allegation beyond authoritative sources;
- no publication merely because a dossier validates.

## Human-centred compression

The standard follows:

`many sources → structured facts → evidence edges → bounded findings → owner synthesis → one recommended next move`

Technical detail belongs in the dossier and validation artifacts. The owner-facing opening stays narrow.
