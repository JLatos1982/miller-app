## Owner synthesis

### What this is

Creekside Withdrawal Management Centre is a current Fraser Health service and a named implementation site within British Columbia’s Road to Recovery expansion. This dossier connects the provincewide commitment to a ten-bed local expansion, current service identity and later utilization reporting.

### What we learned

- The Province announced nearly $154 million over three years for the provincewide Road to Recovery expansion, including 100 beds and regional access pathways.
- Fraser Health later identified Creekside as the site of ten additional withdrawal-management beds, with staffing and transportation support.
- The current Fraser Health directory corroborates Creekside’s continued operation and its Miller curated-resource identity.
- Ministry reporting provides broader Road to Recovery utilization evidence, but the public sources do not allocate a share of the provincewide envelope to Creekside.

### Why it matters

This case shows how Miller can connect a broad policy-and-funding commitment to a verified local resource without incorrectly converting a program envelope into a site-level amount.

### What remains unclear

- What portion, if any, of the nearly $154-million provincewide envelope was allocated to Creekside?
- Are Creekside-specific utilization and capacity measures published after the ten-bed expansion?

### Recommended next move

Keep the Creekside backstory private while seeking a narrow official allocation or implementation record; do not display the provincewide amount as Creekside funding.
