## Fixture — Miller addictions cross-chain and service verification

### Executive summary

A synthesis of 21 policy/accountability chains demonstrates that official implementation evidence can support client, research, and accountability projections without duplicating the underlying records. Twelve of 15 policy-to-service candidates have operational evidence; unresolved identity and governance details remain review gates.

### Scope

The 21 Miller addictions chains, 15 private policy-to-service candidates, curated resource bundle, five private resource candidates, and cited public sources. Miller North was inspected only for architecture compatibility.

### Key findings

- Health-authority service directories are the strongest bridge between policy activity and current service availability.
- Aggregate government totals frequently omit resource-level identity.
- First Nations-led initiatives require funder, governance, operator, and service roles to remain separate.
- Repeated recommendations and partial implementation are review signals, not findings of failure.

### Evidence highlights

Fraser Health supports the Creekside, Hope, and Maple Ridge paths; VCH supports Access Central, Oak Care, and DTES Connections; Island Health and B.C. sources support Inlet Recovery Home; provincial service and utilization pages support the Opioid Treatment Access Line.

### Policy/service relationships

Four exact curated matches, three expansions of existing resources, four direct new-resource candidates, three insufficient-identity services, and one planned unmatched service were identified across all 15 candidates. Red Road North was a separate follow-up discovery.

### Implementation status

Twelve candidates are operationally verified, one is partially verified, and two remain announcement-only. Operational status is not treated as utilization or effectiveness without additional evidence.

### Unresolved questions

- Can operator/intake details be confirmed for the three First Nations service candidates?
- Did the announced Old Massett opening occur?
- Which Budget 2023 aggregate commitments map to named facilities?
- Can future funding observations distinguish capital, operating, one-time, and recurring amounts?

### Method

Deterministic normalization, source-role preservation, exact/bounded identity comparison, explicit-date calculation, and owner-review gates. No model inferred implementation or causality.

### Sources

The fixture retains direct Fraser Health, VCH, Island Health, B.C. government, PHSA, First Nations-led, and regulatory-registry URLs in the structured verification and resource-candidate artifacts.

### Limitations

The original local canonical registry was unseeded during comparison, some service pages lack full identity or intake details, historical URLs can change, and this benchmark does not measure service adequacy or policy effectiveness.
