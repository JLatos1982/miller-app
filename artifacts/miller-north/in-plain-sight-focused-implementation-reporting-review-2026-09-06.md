# In Plain Sight Implementation Reporting — Focused Review

Private owner-review artifact. Review date: 2026-09-06.

## Bottom line

Official sources document substantial implementation activity, but no current comprehensive official status report was located that gives a present, recommendation-by-recommendation status for all 24 recommendations. B.C.'s current Declaration Act Action 3.07 page says the last public comprehensive update was the October 2023 24-month report and that the current framework has limited capacity to capture the breadth and depth of progress across all 24 recommendations. The Ministry says it is exploring a more comprehensive update.

That reporting limitation is a defensible accountability finding. It should be described as a limitation in current public reporting—not as proof that recommendations were ignored, unimplemented or ineffective.

## Status of the remaining recommendations

The remaining recommendations should be classified `not_currently_disaggregated_in_public_reporting` for this fixture. That means this focused review did not find a current official recommendation-level status—not that no work occurred. The October 2023 report is the latest comprehensive public snapshot identified by the Province; later Action 3.07 reporting provides selected highlights rather than a full 24-row status ledger.

## Later official and Indigenous-led material

- Declaration Act reporting records later HPOA implementation, regulator amalgamation, cultural-standard assessment plans and acknowledged capacity/resourcing challenges.
- A June 2025 MMIWG status update describes collective-agreement language, an updated Sharing Concerns process, and a Métis Nation British Columbia bilateral table.
- FNHA's first-anniversary update documents Indigenous-led framework, engagement and standard-development activity, but this bounded search did not locate a later Indigenous-led document that independently assigns a current status to all 24 recommendations.
- No Auditor General audit dedicated to completion of all 24 recommendations was located in this bounded search.

## Reporting assessment

The public record demonstrates activity at several levels: apology and acknowledgement; legal and regulatory change; employment agreement changes; governance/task-team work; education and professional-standard development; funding; complaint-process design; and institutional assessment. It does not provide one current source showing which of all 24 recommendations are complete, partial, underway or unreported.
