# Alberta Indigenous Patient Safety Investigator and Advocate — outcome-transparency review

## What is publicly knowable

Alberta's 2026–29 Primary and Preventative Health Services business plan reports **321 correspondence items in 2025** for the Indigenous Patient Safety Investigator and Advocate. This is a role-specific aggregate activity measure. It is not a count of complaints, unique people, accepted cases, completed reviews or outcomes.

Official sources also support the role's appointment, public access, complaint navigation, delegated Health Charter review function, referral function and ability to provide recommendations or system advice.

## What is only partially knowable

The public mandate describes what the role can do, but not how often each function has been used. Alberta's 2024–25 annual report describes standardized follow-up and information-sharing changes at the parent Office of Alberta Health Advocates level; it does not attribute those changes to this specific role.

## What remains opaque

No comprehensive role-specific public outcome reporting was located for accepted matters, completed reviews, recommendations issued, referrals, resolutions, systemic themes, resulting policy or service changes, or implementation follow-up.

No independent Indigenous-led public evaluation of the role's trust, accessibility, institutional placement, effectiveness or outcomes was located. That absence is not evidence of support or opposition.

## Why the gap matters

The 321 correspondence measure shows public use or contact, but cannot answer what happened after contact. Privacy-protecting aggregate reporting could improve public understanding without exposing complainants or complaint narratives.

## Suggested next bounded action

Prepare, but do not send, a narrow information-request concept to the Ministry of Primary and Preventative Health Services / Office of Alberta Health Advocates for aggregate administrative statistics and non-identifying recommendation follow-up from October 2024 through August 2026. Small cells should be suppressed, and no complaint narratives, patient identities or medical information should be requested.
