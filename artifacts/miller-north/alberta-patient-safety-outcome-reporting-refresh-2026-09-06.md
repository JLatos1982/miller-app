# Alberta Indigenous Patient Safety Investigator and Advocate — bounded outcome-reporting refresh

Date checked: 2026-09-06
Publication state: private research note; no automatic public projection

## Question

Has a public, aggregate, role-specific account appeared for complaints, screening or acceptance, referrals, reviews, recommendations, resolutions, implementation, outcomes, systemic themes or response times?

## Sources and source families checked

- Government of Alberta role, complaint, Health Advocate and Office of Alberta Health Advocates pages
- Alberta Open Government annual-report and business-plan catalogue results
- Alberta Health 2024–25 annual report
- Primary and Preventative Health Services 2026–29 business plan
- Alberta Legislative Assembly Hansard and committee-index results through September 6, 2026
- Targeted First Nations, Indigenous health, Indigenous media and Indigenous-led evaluation searches

## Result

No new role-specific aggregate outcome measure was located.

The strongest quantitative evidence remains **321 correspondence items in 2025**, reported in the Primary and Preventative Health Services 2026–29 business plan. This supports only a correspondence-volume statement. It does not establish 321 unique complainants, accepted cases, investigations, completed reviews, recommendations, referrals, resolutions or outcomes.

The Alberta Health 2024–25 annual report describes improvements at the parent Office of Alberta Health Advocates level. Those statements remain parent-office evidence and are not attributable to the Indigenous Patient Safety Investigator and Advocate without explicit disaggregation.

Legislative debate in March 2026 confirms that correspondence volume is the published performance measure being discussed; it does not add an outcome metric. No independent Indigenous-led public evaluation of role-specific accessibility, trust, independence, recommendations or outcomes was located in the bounded search.

## Reporting gap retained

No comprehensive role-specific public outcome reporting was located for:

- unique complaints or inquiries
- screened, accepted, declined or referred matters
- reviews or investigations initiated or completed
- recommendations issued or accepted
- remedies, resolutions or follow-up
- recurring issue categories or regional patterns
- role-attributed policy or service changes
- response times or annual trends

This is a public-evidence limitation. It does not imply inactivity or ineffectiveness.

## Decision

- Dossier maturity remains `dossier_ready_with_gaps`.
- No publication-safe dossier change is warranted from this refresh.
- No patient-level or complaint-level identifying information was sought or retained.
- Stopping reason: current official reporting avenues were rechecked, the available role-specific metric was already captured, and further open-web searching showed sharply diminishing returns.

## Best next bounded action

If the owner later chooses to pursue it, review the existing draft information-request concept for aggregate administrative counts, non-identifying recommendation follow-up and small-cell suppression. Do not request complaint narratives or patient information.
