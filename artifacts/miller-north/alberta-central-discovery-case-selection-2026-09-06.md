# Alberta case selection — Red Deer and Central Alberta first

> Private discovery decision.

## Red Deer health-care action-plan cultural-safety concerns

Location: Red Deer. Evidence: moderate. Accountability depth: consultation concern but no case-specific investigation or findings. Legal/policy depth: limited. Implementation evidence: not linked to a specific response.

Decision: **rejected** — Too shallow for a substantial accountability dossier.

## Red Deer Regional Hospital Indigenous Hospital Support Services

Location: Red Deer. Evidence: high for operation. Accountability depth: no documented triggering review located. Legal/policy depth: limited. Implementation evidence: current service page.

Decision: **rejected** — Strong service evidence but an unsupported accountability backstory.

## AHS Central Zone Indigenous engagement and cultural-safety activity

Location: Central Zone. Evidence: moderate. Accountability depth: system activity without one coherent findings-to-response chain. Legal/policy depth: limited. Implementation evidence: selected service examples.

Decision: **rejected** — Useful context, not a sufficiently bounded case.

## Mâmawihitowin perinatal cultural-safety intervention

Location: Maskwacîs / Central Alberta. Evidence: moderate to high. Accountability depth: Indigenous-led intervention and evaluation. Legal/policy depth: low. Implementation evidence: research evaluation.

Decision: **reserve** — Strong local learning case but weaker government/legal/accountability depth than the selected panel chain.

## Indigenous Primary Health Care Advisory Panel and implementation

Location: Alberta provincewide, including Treaty 6, 7 and 8 contexts. Evidence: high. Accountability depth: recommendations, formal response, commitments, oversight, funding and implementation. Legal/policy depth: strong governance and Health Charter context. Implementation evidence: multiple current mechanisms.

Decision: **selected** — Only candidate with a defensible multi-stage accountability and implementation chain.

## Selection

Red Deer and Central Alberta searches produced important cultural-safety concerns and current support services, but no equally strong incident-to-investigation-to-response chain. The provincewide panel was selected because it provides 22 recommendations, a formal implementation plan, funding mechanisms, an oversight/complaint function and later follow-up.
