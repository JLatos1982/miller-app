# Miller North Research & Policy product specification

## Product decision

Use one private case projection over existing domain records. Do not create a parallel public database.

## Shared components

1. **Incident or cohort overview** — plain-language subject identity, geography and evidence boundary.
2. **Owner-first synthesis** — one takeaway, three review-first items, five review-next items and a single next move.
3. **Accountability timeline** — dated events with source references.
4. **Evidence path** — source-bearing edges only.
5. **Recommendation tracker** — optional row-level status with a visible non-effectiveness notice.
6. **Government / policy / law** — context scope shown as direct, response, governance or territorial only.
7. **Implementation depth** — acknowledgement, commitment, funding, policy, creation, operation, reporting, evaluation and outcome remain distinct.
8. **Knowledge gaps** — maximum two next questions plus a stopping reason for any follow-up.
9. **Source trail** — authoritative family and role on each source.

## Case-specific components

- B.C.: 24-row thematic recommendation tracker and source-numbering discrepancy notice.
- Saskatchewan: cohort/anonymity banner, court-procedure boundary and later-law context.
- Alberta: candidate-selection rationale, panel recommendation crosswalk and program/advocate mandate boundaries.

## Private preview layout

Case selector → What happened → Why it matters → Timeline → Accountability → Government/policy/law → Implementation → What remains unknown. The preview must have no write, approval or publication controls.
