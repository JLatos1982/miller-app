# In Plain Sight PIDA numbering resolution

> Private owner-review artifact.

## Resolution

The original *In Plain Sight* report is canonical: the health-authority Public Interest Disclosure Act protection is **recommendation 11**. The current Declaration Act Action 3.07 page's association with recommendation 2 is best recorded as an **apparent current reporting-label error**, not renumbering.

## Evidence

- The [original report](https://engage.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/613/2020/11/In-Plain-Sight-Full-Report-2020.pdf) assigns the item to recommendation 11.
- The [2021 government update](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2021HLTH0075-002294), [24-month report](https://news.gov.bc.ca/files/IPS2YearReport.pdf) and [2024 Ministry transition binder](https://docs.openinfo.gov.bc.ca/Ministry_of_Health_Transition_Binder_2024.pdf) also map it to recommendation 11.
- The [current Action 3.07 page](https://declaration.gov.bc.ca/actions/3-07/) labels the item as recommendation 2.

No authoritative renumbering or cross-recommendation scheme was located. This is a high-confidence source-comparison inference; the government page itself has not been corrected by this project.
