# Miller North public-launch readiness review

> Private owner-review artifact. This assesses the current browser experience; it is not a launch authorization.

## Overall status

**needs_revision**

The three-layer product model is coherent and publication-safe, but an Indigenous governance/content review and a reviewed correction channel remain important before a broader launch.

## Ready

- **Clarity and information architecture:** Live Listening, Evidence Library, and Research & Policy now answer different questions and link to one another.
- **Evidence framing:** reported accounts, formal findings, recommendations, implementation evidence, and outcomes are not treated as equivalent.
- **Source transparency:** every Live Listening item and every material Research & Policy claim has a source path.
- **Incident presentation:** 695 approved source-derived rows are shown as 321 evidence groups without altering the underlying records.
- **Privacy boundary:** the Live Listening browser projection excludes names from the 20 incident candidates, ordinary usernames, private excerpts, owner-review reasoning, and internal candidate or incident IDs.
- **Mobile structure:** cards, filters, timelines, recommendation details, and source sections reflow without a required desktop table.

## Needs revision

- **Editorial consistency:** some older Evidence Library summaries retain extraction-oriented wording. Grouping reduces repetition but does not rewrite every legacy summary.
- **Correction channel:** the methodology page explains the planned correction/source-update pathway, but no reviewed submission channel is active.
- **Counts and labels:** the Evidence Library must continue to call its 321 items evidence groups, not distinct incidents.
- **Outcome language:** implementation labels remain vulnerable to being misread as effectiveness ratings; the caution should remain visible wherever statuses appear.

## Owner review

- Four possible duplicate clusters share normalized titles across different URLs and remain unmerged.
- Six existing public-source leads remain withheld because identity or source quality is insufficient for browser publication.
- The 15 anonymized Live Listening items should receive an Indigenous research/governance content review before deployment.
- Facility naming is retained only where it is explicit in public reporting; the owner should confirm that this level of location detail fits the eventual editorial policy.
- Indigenous-led source balance and terminology should be reviewed by someone independent of the implementation team.

## Optional improvements

- Add automated accessibility scanning in addition to keyboard and browser checks.
- Add a reviewed, abuse-resistant correction/source-update channel with no medical-record upload.
- Continue replacing extraction-style legacy summaries with concise editor-reviewed summaries.
- Add a small public change log showing when evidence groups or statuses change.

## Misunderstanding risks and controls

| Risk | Current control |
|---|---|
| A public account is read as a proven allegation | Evidence-state labels and method copy state the distinction directly. |
| A source publication date is read as the event date | Live Listening uses a separate `date_label`; the method page explains date semantics. |
| Several articles are counted as several incidents | Non-destructive presentation grouping and combined source lists. |
| Missing implementation reporting is read as non-implementation | Research & Policy cautions explicitly reject that inference. |
| A legal process is read as a merits finding | Procedural-context explanations remain visible in case pages and methodology. |

## Launch recommendation

Do not treat this build as broadly launch-ready until one Indigenous governance/content review has been completed and the resulting high-priority wording changes are incorporated. The underlying publication-safety and evidence-linkage controls are ready for that review.
