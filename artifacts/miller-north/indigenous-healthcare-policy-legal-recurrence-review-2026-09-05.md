## High-value repeated or continuing signals

1. **In Plain Sight implementation.** The Task Team report and Declaration Action 3.7 describe substantial activity and continuing multi-year work. This supports partial implementation and continued monitoring, not completion of all 24 recommendations.
2. **B.C. complaint pathways.** A later patient-care-quality review again examines openness and cultural safety. The repetition suggests the complaint-system issue remains an active policy problem; it does not prove earlier actions were ignored.
3. **Alberta AHS commitments.** The 2024–25 annual report documents specific actions while continuing to list cultural safety and elimination of racism as active quality objectives. Treat as partial implementation plus ongoing gap reporting.
4. **Alberta human-rights access.** The Indigenous Human Rights Strategy's external review documented barriers to the Commission itself. Later implementation claims should be checked against access/outcome measures, not plan publication.
5. **SHA reconciliation and anti-racism.** A 2019 commitment was reaffirmed in 2024 and followed by a 2025 framework and training work. This is a repeat/continuity signal and implementation evidence; it is not proof the earlier commitment was unimplemented.
6. **Saskatchewan hospital protective services.** The 2026 review follows reported concerns and prior incidents. It is a later related signal. No finding yet ties any reviewed system issue to a specific Miller North proposal.
7. **Forced/coerced sterilization.** Federal parliamentary evidence and responses repeat consent, policy, monitoring and accountability recommendations after the 2017 Saskatoon review. Repetition warrants owner review but does not alone establish that every provincial recommendation remained unimplemented.
8. **Federal Shared Priority 7.** Federal reporting classifies the measure underway and records partner concerns and limited resources alongside funded work. Preserve both evidence streams as partial/continuing, not disputed unless direct contradiction is documented.
