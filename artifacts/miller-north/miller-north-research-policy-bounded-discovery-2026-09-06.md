# Miller North bounded Research & Policy discovery

> Private research artifact. Only publication-safe findings were added to the hidden-site projection.

## British Columbia

- The Ministry of Health's 2026 patient-care-quality review directly connects In Plain Sight to current review of internal patient-safety and patient-initiated quality-review processes. [Patient Care Quality Review](https://engage.gov.bc.ca/govtogetherbc/engagement/patient-care-quality-review/)
- Island Health paused its acute-care Indigenous self-identification program after First Nations feedback and committed to a distinctions-based redesign with shared governance and Indigenous data-governance safeguards. [Temporary Pause of the Indigenous Patient Self-Identification Program in Acute Care](https://medicalstaff.islandhealth.ca/news-events/paused-indgi-self-identi)
- B.C.'s 2026–28 Anti-Racism Action Plan announces a Ministry of Health centre and a September 2027 first progress report. These remain commitments until later evidence demonstrates implementation. [B.C. Anti-Racism Action Plan 2026–28](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026AG0032-000635)

## Saskatchewan

- The First Nations Health Ombudsperson Office published its inaugural report covering July 2023 to March 2025. [Inaugural Report, July 2023–March 2025](https://fnhoo.ca/annual-reports/)
- The Health Minister stated that the Ministry and Saskatchewan Health Authority were reviewing the report and recommendations; no later formal response was located. [October 28, 2025 Hansard](https://docs.legassembly.sk.ca/legdocs/Assembly/Debates/30L2S/20251028DebatesHTML.htm)
- Federal reporting confirms a Saskatchewan Anti-Indigenous Racism Navigator Network, while leaving participants and outcome reporting unclear. [UN Declaration Act Action Plan 2026 reporting — Shared Priority 8](https://justice.canada.ca/eng/declaration/report-rapport/2026/b1.html)

## Alberta

- Current reporting brings the Indigenous Health Division, funding programs, advisory council and advocate together as continuing but distinct response mechanisms. [Reconciliation in Alberta — health actions](https://www.alberta.ca/reconciliation-in-alberta)
- The broader MAPS progress page reports 13 of 30 two-year-plan actions completed and 17 in progress, but does not disaggregate the Indigenous panel's 22 recommendations. [Modernizing primary health care — progress on 2-year plan](https://www.alberta.ca/modernizing-primary-health-care-progress-on-2-year-plan)
- Alberta records 38 anti-racism engagement sessions feeding a planned What We Heard report and strategy; a final strategy was not located. [Reconciliation in Alberta — health actions](https://www.alberta.ca/reconciliation-in-alberta), [Addressing racism in Indigenous health care](https://www.alberta.ca/addressing-racism-in-indigenous-health-care)
