# Research Brief — Legal, policy and governance context for Indigenous healthcare accountability

## Executive summary

This private pilot added formal legal, policy, regulatory, human-rights and Indigenous-governance context to seven Miller North accountability chains. It created 21 source-backed relationships while preserving the difference between law, policy, advice, governance authority, mediated commitments and implementation evidence.

## Scope

The sample covered In Plain Sight, B.C. patient-care quality reform, Alberta's Indigenous Patient Safety Advocate, CPSA reconciliation work, a Saskatchewan human-rights mediation, the Saskatoon coerced-sterilization review and the Saskatchewan First Nations Health Ombudsperson.

## Key findings

- B.C. reports In Plain Sight implementation activity but also says comprehensive reporting across all 24 recommendations is not yet available.
- Alberta's Indigenous Patient Safety Advocate is operational with explicit statutory/mandate limits.
- CPSA anti-racism advice is not a binding standard of practice.
- Bill S-228 received royal assent in 2026, adding a later federal legal stage to the coerced-sterilization context.
- FNHO has directly sourced First Nations governance authority, federal launch funding and current institutional evidence.

## Evidence highlights

- B.C. Declaration Act Action 3.07 and the 24-month provincial update
- Alberta Health Charter and advocate service pages
- Saskatchewan Human Rights Commission anonymized resolution summary
- Parliament's bill-status and committee records
- FNHO governance/annual-report pages and Indigenous Services Canada funding notice

## Policy/service relationships

This track primarily tested policy-to-accountability rather than service linkage. The shared relationship contract still captured governing rules, responses, implementation activity, commitments and institutional creation.

## Implementation status

Implementation ranges from operating mechanisms (Alberta advocate and FNHO), through published regulator outputs, to partial or unresolved reporting. Enactment of Bill S-228 is verified; enforcement and outcomes were not assessed.

## Unresolved questions

- When will full In Plain Sight recommendation-level reporting be available?
- What legal result will follow the B.C. patient-care quality review?
- Is there public follow-up on the Saskatchewan mediation terms?
- What implementation record will follow Bill S-228?

## Method

The pilot selected high-value existing chains, searched registered official source families, classified every relationship and source role, and required a source for every edge. Treaty context was recorded only when directly supported and was separated from statutory rights and policy commitments.

## Sources

## Limitations

This is public-record research, not legal advice. The small pilot does not provide complete legal histories, infer liability or effectiveness, or convert territorial acknowledgements into treaty-right findings.
