## Treaty and Indigenous governance findings

Only the FNHO chain has direct First Nations governance sourcing: the office states that its authority derives from FSIN Chiefs-in-Assembly Resolution 2046 and describes a governance structure accountable through Chiefs-in-Assembly. Its own mission invokes the spirit and intent of Treaty and inherent rights. CPSA's reference to Treaty 6, 7 and 8 territories is classified only as territorial/action-plan context. The other selected chains did not yield a supportable treaty-specific legal conclusion.

## Strongest evidence paths

2. First Nations governance resolution → FNHO → federal launch funding → current office and annual-report access.
3. Saskatoon review → later parliamentary record → enacted Bill S-228, with explicit limits on what the relationship proves.
4. Alberta Health Charter → Indigenous Patient Safety Advocate mandate → operating government contact pathway.
