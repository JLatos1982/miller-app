# Alberta Indigenous primary-care accountability and legal timeline

> Private owner-review artifact.

- 2022-09-29 — Red Deer engagement identifies cultural-safety concerns: Participants raised cultural safety, racism and bias as health-system concerns; this was consultation evidence, not a formal case finding.
- 2022-2023 — Indigenous advisory panel convened: Indigenous leaders and health-system participants developed recommendations for primary health-care reform.
- 2023 — Panel issues 22 recommendations: The final report organizes recommendations around culturally safe, Indigenous-led and accountable care.
- 2023 — Early investment opportunities published: Government identifies early commitments and implementation mechanisms.
- 2024 — The Way Forward published: A separate Indigenous primary-health-care plan sets out implementation work.
- 2024-2026 — Funding and oversight mechanisms operate: Navigator grants, an innovation fund, an Indigenous Health Division and patient-safety advocate are publicly documented.
- 2024-10 to 2025-07 — Anti-racism engagement held: Government gathered input and reported that results were under review; a final resulting strategy was not located.

The chain is advisory and administrative rather than an adjudicated incident case. Program creation and funding are not treated as proof of universal operation or positive outcomes.
