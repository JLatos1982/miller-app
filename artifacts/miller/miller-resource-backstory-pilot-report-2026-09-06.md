# Miller Resource Institutional Backstory Pilot

Private owner-review artifact. Research date: 2026-09-06. Nothing in this report is published or written to the Miller resource registry.

## Result

Six high-value services were tested. All six support complete, source-backed private backstories at the architectural level; three are existing curated resources and three remain private candidates. The pilot created 18 evidence edges. It found three program-level funding links, but no public amount that can safely be attributed to one selected resource.

| Creekside Withdrawal Management Centre | Yes | Road to Recovery commitment → Fraser Health expansion → 10 added beds → current facility page | Operational evidence available | None in this pilot |

| Opioid Treatment Access Line | Yes | Provincial commitment → launch → current access page → utilization | Operational and utilization evidence | Needs a virtual/provincewide Miller resource taxonomy |

## Evidence interpretation

The strongest paths contain three different proofs: a dated policy or funding record, a named operator/service implementation record, and current operation or utilization evidence. A directory listing alone was not treated as proof of the announced opening date. Program funding was retained with an explicit scope note rather than apportioned to individual services.

## Recommended owner action

Use Creekside, Access Central and the Opioid Treatment Access Line as reference backstory patterns. Send Oak Care, Inlet and the OAT line through normal candidate review only after resolving identity/taxonomy questions. Do not publish the backstory layer yet.
