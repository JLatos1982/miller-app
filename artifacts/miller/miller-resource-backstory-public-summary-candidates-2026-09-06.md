Private drafting artifact; not approved or published.

- **Access Central:** Access Central was established as Vancouver Coastal Health's centralized withdrawal-management access pathway under Road to Recovery. Provincial reporting later documented calls and access to beds through the model.
- **Oak Care:** Vancouver Coastal Health moved 18 withdrawal-management beds to Oak Care and increased the program to 21 beds. The current service page confirms access details; the site's address representation and interim status need review before publication.
- **Maple Ridge:** Four youth and young-adult beds were added at Maple Ridge Treatment Centre as part of a wider provincial commitment. Fraser Health continues to list the bed-based program.
- **Inlet:** Official sources identify an eight-bed, Connective-operated recovery home in Port Alberni and Island Health currently lists it. The precise first-intake date should be confirmed before presenting it as fully operational from the announced date.
- **Opioid Treatment Access Line:** B.C. launched a provincewide line for rapid OAT assessment in 2024 and continues to publish access information and utilization evidence. Miller needs a safe virtual-resource representation before publication.
