# First Nations Addictions-Service Policy Chain Review

Date: 2026-09-05
Scope: private, governance-sensitive review

## Chain structure

The strongest observed structure is:

> provincial funding commitment → FNHA/First Nations partnership → local Nation or Nation-alliance governance → named operator or community service → operational or planned evidence

The governance and funding roles must remain separate.

## Verified and planned stages

- **Adams Lake:** four treatment beds were reported open and accepting clients, led by Adams Lake Band Council. The public facility identity and intake route remain insufficient for a Miller resource record.
- **Skidegate:** four supportive-recovery beds at Tll Daagwiiyah Naay were reported open and accepting clients under Skidegate Band Council leadership. Public location, eligibility, and intake details were not located.
- **Old Massett:** four beds at Swan House were expected in July 2026. No later authoritative operational confirmation was located; status is announced, not verified.
- **Red Road North:** the Northern First Nations Alliance identifies the land-based lodge and operator; the provincial Assisted Living Registry lists Red Road North as registered. This is a new private Miller resource candidate.
- **Terrace withdrawal management:** Northern Health purchased a building, but renovations and opening timing were unknown. This is not an operational resource.
- **Braun building recovery beds:** the March source projected ten beds for later spring. This pass did not locate sufficient authoritative operating evidence, so no resource candidate was created.

## Funding and accountability relationships

The benchmark commitment records a $171-million Budget 2023 investment in Indigenous-led services. Later sources document specific projects and beds, but those examples should not be treated as a complete reconciliation of the total fund.

Relevant organizations include local First Nations governments, Northern First Nations Alliance, First Nations Health Authority, CMHA B.C., Northern Health, Interior Health, and the Province. The local Nation or alliance remains the service-governance actor where the source says the service is Nation-led.

## Privacy and governance decisions

- No private patient or deceased-person information was collected.
- No residential address is included unless already published as an operating facility or regulatory record.
- Community-level bed counts are not converted to public resource records without a safe, public intake identity.
- No provincial ownership is inferred from provincial funding.
