# Miller addictions pilot policy chains

Status: private research artifact

## 1. Overdose-prevention services

2016 public-health emergency → Ministerial Order 488 → more than 20 sites documented in early 2017 → provincial minimum service standards in 2025 → 58-site operational footprint reported for September 2025.

This is the cleanest full chain from emergency authority to formal rule, service rollout and utilization reporting.

## 2. Opioid agonist treatment

2018 coroner recommendation → BCCSU clinical guideline updates → pharmacy and nursing standards → nurse prescriber rollout → PharmaCare coverage → 2024 provincewide OAT Access Line → participation/prescriber counts.

Implementation is partial relative to the broad access recommendation because the sources establish activity and reach, not needs-based adequacy or full iOAT access.

## 3. Prescribed alternatives

2021 policy direction → phased programs and dispensing → 2022 coroner recommendation → 2024 PHO review → 2024 Auditor General audit → government response → 2025 revised policy.

The chain demonstrates why “policy adopted” and “program fully implemented” must be separate statuses. Official sources document both delivery and significant gaps.

## 4. Decriminalization

2019 PHO recommendation → 2021 federal expert-task-force recommendations → 2022 federal exemption → January 2023 effective date → September 2023 amendment → May 2024 material restriction → December 2024 implementation summary.

The chain includes three explicit amendment/supersession relationships. No effectiveness conclusion is made.

## 5. Treatment capacity / Road to Recovery

2018/2022 continuum recommendations → 2022 Adult System of Care Framework → 2023 Budget commitment → Vancouver launch → 2024 provincewide expansion commitment → Fraser, Island and local service evidence in 2026.

Named operational services can be linked to Miller candidates, while overlapping provincial bed totals remain unresolved.

## 6. Supportive-recovery regulation

2018 coroner recommendation for treatment/recovery oversight → 2019 Assisted Living Regulation → 2021 supportive-recovery standards and implementation resources → later VCH contract expectations.

This supports partial implementation. Whether the framework fully meets the coroner recommendation is owner-review territory.

## 7. First Nations-led treatment capacity

2023 Indigenous-led services fund → FNHA network/evaluation evidence → Terrace property and planned capacity → 2026 openings in Adams Lake, Skidegate and Old Massett.

The chain stays in Miller’s addictions domain. Its Indigenous-specific service context does not turn these records into Miller North accountability records.
