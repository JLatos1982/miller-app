# Miller Practical Supports — targeted gap and connectivity review

Private owner artifact. Production Miller resources were read only.

## Coverage before the targeted pass

The publication-safe view had 27 supports: housing 7, advocacy/navigation 6, employment 4, transportation 3, ID 2, income/benefits 2, training 2 and basic needs 1.

Housing had reasonable breadth across emergency shelter, rent-bank, registry and outreach/navigation functions. The meaningful thin areas were basic needs, ID help, income/benefit navigation, training and transportation. Surrey and Maple Ridge/Pitt Meadows also had fewer direct practical access points than Burnaby or New Westminster.

## Targeted additions

Four current, source-backed programs were added to the publication-safe projection:

| Program | Area | Category | Match decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Westminster Crises Response Outreach Team | New Westminster | ID / outreach | `new_resource_candidate` |
| SHARE Food Bank | Tri-Cities | Basic needs | `existing_org_new_program` — Miller already has a broader SHARE organization record |
| StreetSMART | Surrey City Centre / Newton | Advocacy/navigation | `existing_org_new_program` — distinct City/Library/Lookout partnership program |
| Alouette Outreach Services | Maple Ridge / Pitt Meadows | Advocacy/navigation | `new_resource_candidate` |

No canonical Miller resource was created or altered. Public display uses the existing read-only projection.

## Coverage after the targeted pass

31 publication-safe supports: advocacy/navigation 8, housing 7, employment 4, transportation 3, ID 3, basic needs 2, income/benefits 2 and training 2.

The pass improves direct ID help in New Westminster, basic-needs access in the Tri-Cities, Surrey outreach/navigation and Maple Ridge/Pitt Meadows outreach. Income/benefit navigation, training and transportation remain the thinnest categories and are the next targeted search priorities.

## Related-support connections

Every practical-support category now receives at most two modest “You may also need” category suggestions. These are navigation relationships—not claims that organizations are affiliated or that a particular care plan is appropriate.

Current examples:

- housing → income/benefits; advocacy/navigation
- employment → training; transportation
- ID → income/benefits; housing
- basic needs → housing; income/benefits

Cards also surface at most two current/open Funding & Assistance records using purpose-based matching and the label “Possible funding/support to explore.” Eligibility is not implied, and closed or archived funding is excluded.

## Remaining gaps

1. More locally accessible benefits advocacy outside Vancouver.
2. More direct, current transport assistance for appointments and employment, especially beyond disability- or age-specific programs.
3. More training programs with current intake and a clear public access page.
4. Better separation of supportive, transitional and recovery-oriented housing where the existing treatment registry does not already cover the service.
5. Periodic source checks for city outreach schedules, which can change faster than provincewide benefit pages.
