# Miller Practical Supports benchmark

66 private candidate records were assembled: 60 exact existing Miller resource matches and 6 new or program-level candidates requiring reconciliation. Categories: housing 15; employment 11; training_education 8; transportation 8; legal_advocacy 8; basic_needs 7; navigation_outreach 6; identification 2; income_benefits 1.

The benchmark deliberately treats existing directory presence as identity evidence, not proof that hours, eligibility, or intake remain current. 35 records have Lower Mainland or Fraser-region relevance.
