## Funding & Assistance

Five authoritative programs were added, bringing the Miller funding projection from 7 to 12:

- B.C. Security Deposit Supplement — recurring, verify eligibility
- B.C. Moving, Transportation and Living Costs Supplement — recurring, pre-approval generally required
- B.C. Crisis Supplement — recurring eligibility-based assistance
- Transportation to residential alcohol and drug treatment — recurring eligibility-based assistance
- Canada Disability Benefit — current application route open

Freshness states now contain 11 recurring records and one open record. The open benefit is scheduled for a weekly check; recurring records remain on a monthly cadence. All cards continue to direct readers to official pages and say to verify before applying.

## Visual product work

Practical Supports and Miller Funding & Assistance now share Miller’s white/light-blue surface, blue controls, Trebuchet typography and card language. The existing dog component enters once across the bounded hero and rests at the right. It is decorative, ignores pointer input, becomes static for reduced-motion users and is removed on short-height, landscape-tablet and small-screen layouts.

## Remaining gaps

- Basic-needs coverage is still thin.
- Training options remain less locally specific than housing and employment.
- The support projection needs a longer-term canonical enrichment/review workflow.
- The email provider remains dependent on server-side Resend configuration; this pass did not add or expose credentials.

## Recommended next move

Run a short owner review of the ten new support cards in the real browser, then decide which should become canonical Miller resources versus remain navigation-only projection records.
