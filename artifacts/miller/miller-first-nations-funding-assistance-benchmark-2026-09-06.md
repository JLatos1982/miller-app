# First Nations Funding & Assistance benchmark

Private, review-gated benchmark. 41 current, recurring, periodic, or explicitly verify-before-applying program records were structured from official sources. Coverage: British Columbia 11; Federal 10; Alberta 15; Saskatchewan 5.

## Strongest practical opportunities

- FNHA Health Benefits and Medical Transportation provide durable, directly navigable individual supports in B.C.
- ISC Post-Secondary Student Support and ISET are recurring federal program frameworks delivered through First Nations or Indigenous service-delivery organizations.
- Alberta's official Indigenous funding registry is the strongest single provincial program index, with health, housing, training, business, and community streams.
- Saskatchewan Indian Equity Foundation and Saskatchewan's official business-funding directory provide a strong business-financing seam; practical individual assistance is less centrally indexed.

## Safeguard

Funding source is not governance authority. Records default to verify-before-applying unless an authoritative page establishes an open, recurring, or scheduled intake.
