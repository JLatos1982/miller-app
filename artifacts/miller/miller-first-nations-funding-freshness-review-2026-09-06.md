# First Nations funding freshness review

- Last verified for this benchmark: 2026-09-06.
- Durable benefits/directories: monthly automated link check plus quarterly content review.
- Periodic grants and bursaries: verify monthly while intake is open or upcoming; otherwise every 90 days.
- One-time announcements: retain only as closed historical context, never as current opportunity.

- An unchanged webpage does not prove funds remain available; use **verify before applying** when the current intake cannot be established.
