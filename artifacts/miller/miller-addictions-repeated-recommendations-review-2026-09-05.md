# Miller addictions repeated and recurrent recommendation review

Status: private owner-review artifact

Seven modeled commitments carry a repeat-recommendation signal.

| Theme | Earlier signal | Later signal | Conservative interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treatment/recovery regulation | 2018 death review panel | 2022 panel says prior recommendations remain relevant and required | Regulatory activity occurred, but the later panel suggests unresolved work. |
| OAT/iOAT access | 2017 inquest and 2018 panel | 2022 continuum recommendation | Access expanded; later repetition may reflect scale or coverage gaps. |
| Safer alternatives | 2019 PHO / 2021 policy | 2022 and 2023 panels | Prescription programs operated; later recommendations sought broader access. |
| Evidence-based continuum | 2018 panel | 2022 panel and 2022 framework | Framework and services exist; provincewide completion remains unverified. |
| Decriminalization/public-health approach | 2019 PHO | 2021 federal task force | Limited B.C. implementation followed, not national implementation. |
| National public-health drug framework | 2021 expert task force | 2023 CDSS and later provincial recommendations | Some strategy themes recur; the proposed comprehensive framework was not fully identified. |
| Public performance reporting | 2021 policy monitoring promise | 2024 Auditor General findings | Monitoring existed, while public reporting was assessed as insufficient. |

Later mortality, later complaints or renewed recommendations are not coded as proof that a policy failed. They are stored as `repeated_recommendation`, `continued_gap_reported`, or `later_related_signal_found` for bounded follow-up.

Owner review should focus on statements that compare the scope of a later instrument to an earlier recommendation, especially treatment regulation, non-prescribed alternatives and national standards.
