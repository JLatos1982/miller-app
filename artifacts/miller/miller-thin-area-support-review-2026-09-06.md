# Miller practical-support thin-area review

The targeted pass raised the public Practical Supports projection from **31 to 43 records** without changing Miller's canonical resource database.

- Income / benefits: 2 → 5
- Training: 2 → 6
- Transportation: 3 → 8

Twelve current records were added and three existing publication-safe cards were enriched. The matching pass avoided three likely presentation duplicates: general DABC benefit support remained one card, Sources regional advocacy remained one card, and TaxiSaver was described within the existing HandyDART/HandyCard card.

The strongest additions are Disability Alliance BC's Tax AID program, Plan Institute's Disability Planning Helpline, current Lower Mainland employment-training programs, B.C.'s Travel Assistance Program, Hope Air, the B.C. assistance medical-transport supplement and FNHA Medical Transportation.

The most important remaining gaps are local human benefits navigation in Tri-Cities and Maple Ridge/Pitt Meadows, cohort freshness for training programs, and broadly accessible transportation help for people who do not meet medical, disability, age or First Nations eligibility rules.
