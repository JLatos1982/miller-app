## Recommendation extraction

Thirty commitments are modeled in the fixture. Thirteen originate in coroner, PHO, expert-task-force or audit recommendations; the remainder are formal government, regulatory or service commitments.

The clearest implemented recommendation is provincial drug checking: a 2018 recommendation is followed by a 2021 distributed service and a 2024 official count of 128 access sites. This is evidence of implementation, not proof that access is adequate in every community.

The clearest partial histories are OAT access, prescribed alternatives, treatment oversight and the continuum of care. Each has concrete operational evidence plus later official documentation of remaining gaps or repeated recommendations.

## Response documents

The BC Coroners Service report index exposes ministry and regulator response letters for the 2022 panel. These are retained as response-stage sources. They prove acknowledgement and stated action, not completion. Commitment-level extraction from those letters remains owner-gated.

## Privacy

This review uses aggregate/systemic reports. It does not reproduce unnecessary names or circumstances of individual deceased people.
