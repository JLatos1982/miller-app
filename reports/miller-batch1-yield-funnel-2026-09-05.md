# Miller Batch 1 yield funnel

The clean audited post-fix Batch 1 had 25 records. It was not a high-yield public-facility cohort.

| Stage | Count | Note |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Input | 25 | Original mixed missing-location batch |
| Protected or intentionally non-map | 5 | 3 protected classifications plus 2 generic/non-physical records |
| Ordinary public-fixed candidates | 8 | Public-fixed recovery classifications; some still have weak program identity |
| Candidate civic address found | 18 | Includes conflicting/parent-only addresses, not all usable |
| Geocoded | 8 | Conflicts are deliberately not geocoded/published |
| Program-location identity confirmed | 4 | Keystone, Vernon OAT, Mollie's Place, Oak Counselling |
| Stageable | 2 | Mollie's Place and Oak Counselling |
| Owner accepted | 2 | Both were applied through QC and published |

Largest drop-off: program-location identity and multi-site/source-context conflict. The apparent 2/25 yield is misleading; it is 2/8 among records classified public-fixed, and only 4/8 had a confirmed program-at-site relationship. Future batches should be pre-screened for named, municipality-specific, ordinary client-facing facilities and exclude generic categories, multi-site services, protected housing, virtual services, and organization-only records.
